AI running in your browser

OpenAI Whisper (speech → text), T5/BART (summarization) and OPUS-MT (translation) are compiled to WebAssembly and executed on your device with ONNX Runtime Web. Nothing you type, say, or upload ever leaves this tab.

  • 🔒 Private — data never leaves the device
  • 💰 Free — no servers, no tokens
  • ⚡ One-click — loads once, works offline
  • 📱 Installable — a real app from your browser
